# Academic Framework Reference

## Primary Framework: METI DX Promotion Index
- **Full name (JP):** DX推進指標とそのガイダンス
- **Publisher:** 経済産業省 (Ministry of Economy, Trade and Industry — METI)
- **Published:** 2019 / Updated guidance: 2022
- **Official URL:** https://www.meti.go.jp/policy/it_policy/investment/dx-checklists.html
- **Purpose:** Self-assessment tool for Japanese companies to evaluate DX readiness

## Why This Framework (Academic Justification)
1. Government-issued → highest legitimacy for Japanese academic audience
2. Designed for all company sizes including SMEs (中小企業対応)
3. Two analytical axes: Business/Management + IT Systems/Data
4. Used as baseline in peer-reviewed DX research in Japan (Waseda, Hitotsubashi, Tohoku)
5. Directly cited in Japan's DX White Paper (DX白書) by IPA

## Maturity Levels — Exact METI Definitions
| Level | JP Label     | EN Label              | Description |
|-------|--------------|-----------------------|-------------|
| 0     | 未着手        | Not started           | No DX awareness or initiatives |
| 1     | 一部着手      | Partially initiated   | Ad hoc, isolated experiments |
| 2     | 検討中        | Under consideration   | Planning expansion, some cross-dept |
| 3     | 全社展開      | Company-wide          | Systematic, organization-wide |
| 4     | トップランナー | Industry leader       | Global benchmark, continuous innovation |

## Five Dimensions — Weights Used in This Agent
| # | Dimension (EN)              | Dimension (JP)          | Weight |
|---|-----------------------------|-------------------------|--------|
| 1 | Strategy & Vision           | 戦略・ビジョン            | 20%    |
| 2 | Business Process            | 業務プロセス              | 30%    |
| 3 | Organization & Human Capital| 組織・人材                | 20%    |
| 4 | Digital Foundation & Data   | デジタル基盤・データ       | 20%    |
| 5 | Governance & Risk           | ガバナンス・リスク管理     | 10%    |

**Note:** Business Process weighted highest (30%) to align with research focus
on AI automation of operational processes in SMEs.

## Benchmark Data Source

Used for relative positioning in the diagnostic report.
